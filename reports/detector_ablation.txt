7. SCIENTIFIC INTERPRETATION & ANSWERS TO QUESTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Does AE improve Mahalanobis?
   NO. Combining AE with Mahalanobis decreases AUROC from 0.7450 to 0.6967 and AUPRC
   from 0.8257 to 0.8003, while increasing known-event FPR from 10.0% to 15.0%.
   AE reconstruction errors on normal events degrade precision.

B. Does Energy improve Mahalanobis?
   NO. Adding Energy decreases AUROC from 0.7450 to 0.7083, AUPRC from 0.8257 to 0.7460,
   and overall recall from 40.0% to 30.0% (SLSN recall drops from 100% to 70%).
   High classifier overconfidence on extreme flux transients drives Energy scores near zero,
   artificially dampening Mahalanobis anomaly detection.

C. Does the full ensemble improve over Mahalanobis?
   NO. The full ensemble achieves AUROC 0.6950 (vs 0.7450), AUPRC 0.7412 (vs 0.8257),
   F1 0.4889 (vs 0.5455), and doubles the known-event FPR from 10.0% to 20.0%.

D. Which configuration gives the best AUROC?
   Mahalanobis only (AUROC = 0.7450).

E. Which configuration gives the best AUPRC?
   Mahalanobis only (AUPRC = 0.8257).

F. Which configuration gives the best F1?
   Mahalanobis only (F1 = 0.5455).

G. Which gives the best TPR at 10% or lower FPR, if measurable?
   Mahalanobis only achieves 40.0% TPR at <=10% FPR (specifically, at exactly 10% FPR,
   Mahalanobis achieves 40.0% recall, compared to 26.7% for the current ensemble).

H. Does any added detector provide meaningful complementary information?
   Minimally. Out of 30 anomalies, AE uniquely rescued 1 LRN event (LRN_0015), but at the
   cost of adding 2 false positives on normal events. Energy rescued 1 TDE event (TDE_0026)
   while actively suppressing 2 SLSN detections.

I. Is there evidence that Mahalanobis should become the primary anomaly signal?
   YES. Mahalanobis distance strictly dominates all configurations across AUROC (0.7450),
   AUPRC (0.8257), F1 (0.5455), Accuracy (0.6000), Precision (0.8571), and FPR (10.0%).
   However, the AUROC advantage (+0.0500 vs ensemble) is not yet statistically significant
   at alpha=0.05 (bootstrap p = 0.2880) due to test sample size N=50.

J. Should Energy be retained, down-weighted, or investigated separately?
   Energy should be DOWN-WEIGHTED or investigated separately. In its current form, closed-world
   logits without temperature scaling or energy calibration produce negative rank correlation
   with ground-truth anomalies (rho = -0.0962). Retaining it at 25% degrades ensemble quality.
