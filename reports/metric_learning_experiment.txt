================================================================================
Objective: Test whether Supervised Contrastive (SupCon) regularization on the
           128-D lightcurve embedding improves OOD representation quality.
Formulation: L_total = L_CE + lambda * L_SupCon (lambda = 0.10, tau = 0.10)
Strict Constraint: Metric loss applied ONLY to known training labels.
                   No held-out anomaly labels (LRN, SLSN, TDE) influenced training.
Benchmark: Exact 50-event benchmark (20 known, 30 anomalies), threshold = 0.65.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. SCIENTIFIC FINDINGS & INTERPRETATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. OOD Ranking Improvement:
   Adding SupCon regularization (lambda = 0.10) improves Lightcurve AUROC from 0.8050
   to 0.8433 (+0.0383) and AUPRC from 0.8686 to 0.8907 (+0.0221).
   Average Precision increases from 0.8712 to 0.8930.

2. False Positive Rate Reduction:
   The known-event false alarm rate at threshold 0.65 is halved from 10.0% to 5.0%.
   SupCon constrains known classes into compact clusters, reducing boundary leakage.

3. Manifold Separation of Held-Out Anomalies:
   - LRN mean distance increases from 8.9842 to 10.9515 (+1.9673).
   - TDE mean distance increases from 7.4586 to 8.5415 (+1.0829).
   By pulling known classes inward, metric learning creates a wider buffer zone between
   the known manifold and non-conforming out-of-distribution transients.
